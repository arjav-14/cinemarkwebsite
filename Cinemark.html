Our Story

We began with one belief — every city deserves a world-class cinema experience.

Today, Cinemark Movies creates destinations where people celebrate birthdays, watch blockbusters, savour gourmet food and make lifelong memories — filling the gap between the basic single-screen theatre and the unaffordable luxury multiplex. Not just cinemas. Landmarks, where a city gathers.

Why Cinema Will Never Die

Every generation has announced the death of cinema. Every generation has been wrong.

It survivedTelevision
It survivedVideo & DVD
It survivedStreaming
It survivedA pandemic

Because a cinema was never really about the film. It is about the girl who saw her first big screen and never forgot it. The family that goes every Diwali. The friends who queue at midnight. The proposal in row H. You cannot download that.

A screen at home plays a film.
A cinema gives a city somewhere to go.

Questions, Answered

What partners ask us first.

Five stages: a site visit where our team evaluates catchment, footfall, layout and regulatory viability · design & fit-out to Cinemark specification · staff hiring with Phase I & II training · end-to-end project monitoring · and a grand opening with a full launch campaign. From day one after launch, the monthly Co-Operational Working begins.

Franchisee-Owned, Franchisee-Operated. You own the cinema and run it — hiring your team and retaining operational profits — while Cinemark provides the brand, SOPs, centralized supply chain, technology, training and monthly co-operational support. You own it, you run it, you earn it.

Entrepreneurs, business families, developers and operators who want a high-growth branded business they run themselves. A background in hospitality, F&B, retail or events is an advantage — what matters most is an entrepreneurial mindset and a strong local network. No cinema experience is needed; the training programme is designed exactly for that.

From a minimum of 8,000 sq ft (Cinemark Classic) up to 12,000 sq ft (Cinemark Luxe), with ceiling heights from 12 ft up to 25 ft. High-footfall locations — market hub, mall, high street or standalone — with good visibility, frontage and parking work best. Our team evaluates your property during the site visit.

Your team completes 6–10 days of HQ training and 15 days of on-site launch support. Most FOFO Cinemark locations reach full operational independence within 1–3 months of launch — backed by a 24/7 franchise helpline and refresher training twice a year.

Yes — owned property is often the strongest starting point. We also work with leased spaces, mall units and land yet to be developed. Share your layout in the enquiry form and our team will assess its suitability against Cinemark's site requirements.

15 years — a long-term, stable horizon to recover, grow and compound your investment, with an active partner beside you throughout. Detailed terms and customised financial projections are shared personally during the franchise evaluation process.
